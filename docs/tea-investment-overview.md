# TEA investment overview

The overview starts with six saved-model metrics: CAPEX, annual cash OPEX range, last modelled operating-year revenue, NPV, IRR and discounted payback. The OPEX range is explicitly the observed min–max across saved operating years, not uncertainty. CAPEX has no fabricated range. Currency and historical basis remain visible.

Horizontal analysis sections cover capital composition plus equipment purchase-cost contribution, annual fixed/variable cash OPEX plus operating cost drivers, and payment-method revenue. Product sales and commercial payments use the same selected operating year. The financial ledger supplies the annual/cumulative cash-flow chart.

A single Assumptions & methods explorer contains Engineering, Capital, Operations, Revenue, Financial model and Study controls & reviews. Nested folders expose saved assumptions, source records and calculation results. Existing specialist actions, export links, revenue interpretation, review questions, exclusions and version controls remain available in Study controls & reviews. No baseline is modified by browsing. Important missing results and priced-scope exclusions remain visible above the snapshot.
