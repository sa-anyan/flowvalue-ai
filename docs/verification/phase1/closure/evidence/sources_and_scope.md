# Independently checked source ledger — FV-P1-EVIDENCE-001

Access/reconciliation date: 11 November 2026. These are independent references, **not recovered original implementation sources**. Existing PDF page images/hashes are retained in the parent sizing evidence. No third-party code or textbook tables were copied.

- [NIST SP811 Appendix B.9](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-appendix-b-conversion-factors/nist-guide-si-appendix-b9) and [B.8](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-appendix-b-conversion-factors/nist-guide-si-appendix-b8): independent unit definitions and quantity distinctions. B.9 includes velocity, viscosity and heat-transfer-coefficient conversion factors; their publication does not mean FlowValue implements them. Exact inch/pound/gallon/time definitions independently reconstruct the named checks; no production factors are used as the expected-value oracle.
- [DOE HDBK-1012 volume2](https://www.energy.gov/documents/doe-hdbk-1012-92vol2), printed39/PDF61, and [ASHRAE Fundamentals 2021 Chapter4](https://handbook.ashrae.org/Handbooks/F21/IP/F21_Ch04/F21_Ch04_ip.aspx), heat-exchanger equations43–44/discussion: thermal area identity and corrected mean driving force. A chosen U or F still needs a project basis. The ASHRAE chapter explicitly separates boiling/condensation topics; it does not supply a universal vaporizer U.
- [DOE HDBK-1012 volume1](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/DOE-HDBK-1012-92_VOL1.pdf), printed44/PDF70: phase enthalpy definitions. The duty calculation is an independently stated steady first-law rearrangement with supplied properties, not a complete process balance.
- [EPA-600/2-77-152, Chen and Miele, September1977](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=20014PLJ.TXT), AppendixA: resin capacity expressed per resin volume, including dependence on resin/chemical conditions. It corroborates the capacity definition, not this engine's working-capacity selection or breakthrough prediction.
- [EPA PFAS webinar, 30April2024](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2024-05/epa-sdws-pfas-regtrmt-webinar_slides_april-30-2024.pdf), PDF52: bed contact time and loading definitions. Vbed=Q×EBCT and A=Q/loading are rearrangements. Vessel fill allowance is explicit. Listed treatment examples are not adopted as universal design bounds. [EPA treatment technologies overview](https://www.epa.gov/sdwa/overview-drinking-water-treatment-technologies), ion-exchange sections, additionally supports treatment-specific resin/bed assumptions.
- [TU Dresden, David Kneis, 4November2025, Residence times](https://wwwpub.zih.tu-dresden.de/~kneis/teaching/sysan/04_residenceTimes/04_residenceTimes.html): V/Q residence identity and variation of individual particle residence. The volume/fill rearrangement is an engineering envelope; kinetics and gas/particle drying are separate.
- [NASA Glenn compressor thermodynamics](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/airplane/compth.html): ideal constant-specific-heat compression and efficiency, using total states. It supports Z=1 only. The engine's multiplicative Z is not a rigorous nonideal-path model, and static/total conditions need consistent interpretation.
- [NASA equation of state](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/equation-of-state/) supplies the ideal-gas identity. [University of Kiel, Compression factor, equation1.9](https://www.tf.uni-kiel.de/matwis/amat/td_kin_i/kap_1/backbone/r_se6.html) defines Z, supporting the actual-volume rearrangement with a qualified state-specific Z. NASA's temperature-offset examples are not used as a conversion authority. IUPAC Gold Book term C01216 was located in search, but direct retrieval returned403; it is not treated as an inspected primary source.
- [University of Massachusetts moisture/total-solids teaching page](https://people.umass.edu/~mcclemen/581Moisture.html): moisture and solids basis. The water-removal equation is independently derived by conserving dry solids; no edition/date or original code lineage is asserted.
- [DOE/Hydraulic Institute, Improving Pumping System Performance, Second Edition](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2014/05/f16/pump.pdf), NPSH discussion PDF42: pump/system suitability and cavitation need more than a finite power result. The pressure-work identity and historical coordinate conversion are independently derived dimensionally; the sourcebook is not claimed as the original costing-coordinate source. No blanket NPSH margin is imported.
- [NPTEL Module10, Lecture35](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/storage2/courses/103104043/lecture35/35_2.htm) and [INFLIBNET filtration teaching chapter](https://ebooks.inflibnet.ac.in/ftp02/chapter/mechanical-separation-2-filtration/): liquid/solid cake resistance and time dependence explain why hydraulic-loading area is not a complete filter design. No diagram coefficients are transcribed.
- [EPA CETRED, EPA530-R-94-014, May1994 draft](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=10000SO7.TXT), AppendixA.6, printedA-38–A-40: spray-dryer absorber droplet/wall-deposition constraints. This is contextual applicability evidence, not a generic food-dryer design authority or a published basis for arbitrary gas residence time. No example residence/geometry range is promoted into a universal default.
