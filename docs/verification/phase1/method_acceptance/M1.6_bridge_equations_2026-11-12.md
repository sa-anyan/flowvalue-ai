# M1.6 · Bridge equations as verified methods (roadmap finding #11)

**Date:** 12 November 2026 · **Owner:** Engineer 1 · **Roadmap:** rev 1.1 M1.6; section 9.2 finding #11

The engineering-review register (section 9.2, finding #11) listed five equations inside the conversion bridges that were classed as adapters, not Phase 1 methods: LMTD, enthalpy duty, pump head, gas flow and drying load. This record gives each one independent reference evidence.

Every expected value below was calculated by hand from the textbook relationship before the test was run. Two of my own first hand calculations were off in the fourth significant figure; I corrected them by recalculating, not by reading FlowValue's output.
