## Scope of the decision

- Independent verification of **every** production method is P1 acceptance (paragraph117/G1), not an invented extra G0 requirement. R-02 remains High future-method risk; documenting the gap/reference policy is the P0 obligation.
- Equipment-index/source verification, justified pricing coverage and purchased/installed scope are P3. R-03 is documented now; no coefficients or CPI logic were changed.
- The exact13-field contract is a P4 requirement (paragraph213). Its unspecified names prevent complete compatibility certification; agreement is required before schema work, not a mandate to delete current fields.
- Full live tenant isolation, backup objectives and release-level security assessment belong to P8. G0 requires triage of known security issues, not an unsupported claim of enterprise certification.
- Formal reference-project agreement is M0.5 now. Industrial pilot validation is P9 later. A proposal or university example does not establish either.
