SKIPPED VARIANTS AND PARTIAL COVERAGE
====================================
- Activated-carbon adsorbers (printed p. 256): Exact 10,000 lb carbon branch boundary is unassigned in the printed inequalities and remains rejected. Outer extrapolation uses a disclosed assumed exponent.
- Agitators (printed p. 257): Agitator reference-to-target scaling across exponent bands is blocked.
- Blowers (printed p. 261): Below-3-psi variable-exponent blower curve.
- Chimneys/stacks (printed p. 266): Short stacks: no numerical exponent.
- Columns (printed p. 268): Diameter/per-height column graph is not the weight-based cost method.
- Coolers/quenchers (printed p. 274): Cascade cooler and dilution-air-port graph-only variants.
- Motors/drives (printed p. 293): Large electric motors and variable-speed drives: no separately specified base-price exponent.
- Pipe/pipelines (printed p. 294): Bare pipe, slurry pipeline and submarine pipeline curves: no separately specified exponent. Fittings/valves are factors only.
- Centrifugal pumps (printed p. 296): Conventional and inline centrifugal pumps: exponent cells are blank.
- Miscellaneous pumps (printed p. 297): Sliding-vane curve: no distinct printed exponent. Chemical-injection flow unit unresolved: identical documented source basis required.
- Pressure vessels (printed p. 298): 250-psi volume and horizontal/vertical weight curves: no printed exponent.
- Size enlargement (printed p. 302): Screw extrusion and disk/drum granulation: factors only; no separate base-price method.

IMPORTANT PRICE SCOPES AND BASIS
===============================
- Direct-price formulas retain historical early-1987 USD, CE Index 320.
- Reference scaling preserves the documented reference currency, date, index and configuration.
- Ducts: price per foot, not total duct cost.
- Column packing: price per cubic foot, not total packing/equipment cost.
- Pipelines: installed price per foot, scaled by pipe size; not total pipeline cost.
- Grinding mills distinguish installed and purchased price scopes.
- Reference-price methods require a documented comparable price and reviewed size interval.
- Source gallon/ton definitions and unresolved chemical-injection flow basis are retained explicitly.
- Out-of-range targets use nearest-boundary anchors and are labelled unvalidated extrapolations.
