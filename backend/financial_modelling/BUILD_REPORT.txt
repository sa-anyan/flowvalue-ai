PROVISIONAL CONVENTIONS / REMAINING VALIDATION
- Annual timing, not monthly/XNPV; dates prorate physical operation only.
- One project-currency term facility; no capitalized/rolled-up construction interest,
  floating-rate/multiple-facility refinancing or foreign-debt remeasurement.
- Shared preliminary book/tax depreciation; no deferred-tax accounting.
- MACRS applies supplied tables only; eligibility, special recapture/bonus rules
  and legal filing validation remain external.
- Generic flat annual tax policies; no automatic jurisdictional rates, VAT, loss
  carryback, refundable/carrying tax credits or credit eligibility checks.
- Time-zero expensed startup uses a model loss bucket, not a legal tax-year return.
- Break-even is annual zero-PBT, not project NPV-zero optimization.
- Multi-product unit economics require explicit allocation and are unavailable
  where it is missing; no arbitrary byproduct credits or mixed-unit sums.
- Nonconventional uniform-flow IRR uses disclosed bounded candidates and withholds
  a unique rate. Ill-conditioned numerical cases may be unavailable.
- Financial scenarios do not silently rerun upstream cost/revenue correlations;
  dependent source snapshots require explicit refresh or documented actual overrides.
- Only Garrett is registered; future profiles must use the same engine and ledger.
- Snapshots/actor labels are not authentication, signed approval or database history.
- No frontend, dashboard, export redesign, deployment or live API binding added.
Industrial calibration, accounting/tax review and broader independent validation
remain outstanding. Software test success is not certification or accreditation.
