FLOWVALUE AI — REVENUE ESTIMATION ENGINE
Date: 30 October 2026

DELIVERED
Garrett-guided modular revenue engine in backend/revenue.
Main/by-product records reference explicit physical outlet stream IDs.
Production, allocation, saleable quality, quantities sold and unsold quantities
are separate. Nothing automatically treats every outlet as revenue.
Shared annual hours/throughput schedule; explicit product-specific annual sales
methods, prices, market demand/growth and commercial adjustments.
Annual revenue = sum(quantity sold * net unit selling price).
Net FOB/gross price terms, included charges, additional charges, premiums,
deductions and separate unapplied OPEX charges are explicit and traceable.
Duplicate allocation, duplicate/hierarchical deductions and deductions already
included in net prices are blocked. No product purchasing costs are added.
