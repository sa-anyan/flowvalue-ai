## Sizing bridge

`mass_residence` computes V=(mass flow/density)*residence/working fraction;
`mass_to_volume_flow` computes Q=mass flow/density. Source mass flow must be an
unambiguous canonical kg/h observation. Density must be an explicit positive,
finite engineering assumption in kg/m3; residence/working fraction are independently
validated. The LLM explains assumptions/uncertainty, and Python performs arithmetic.
No default density, pressure, head, duty or capacity is silently supplied.

Missing pump head/power, exchanger area/duty, unconfirmed identities or unsupported
curve coordinates still require review. This repair makes the handoff run; it does
not certify all equipment designs or convert unknown sizing into supported prices.
